## Add a study

Add the paper to `docs/source/theory/references.bib`, then one row to
`docs/source/about/applications.yaml` with its BibTeX key and one sentence on what
HydroModPy did. Open a pull request, or write to the contacts of
{doc}`Work with us <work-with-us>`. Name a site only when the paper names it.

To cite HydroModPy itself, see {doc}`Cite <cite>`. The references behind the equations and
the methods are in the {doc}`bibliography of the theory </theory/bibliography>`.
