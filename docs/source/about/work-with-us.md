# Work with us

HydroModPy is open research software under the EPL-2.0 licence. You can install it and run it
on your own catchment without asking anyone. Write to the team when you want to collaborate,
use it for a study, or report a problem.

## Ask for a study or a collaboration

The team works in research. A request for a study on your territory is read by the two
contacts above, who say whether and how the team can take it on. To help them answer, say:

1. **The question.** What you want to know: where the water table is, which streams keep
   flowing in a dry year, what a change of climate or of use does.
2. **The place.** The catchment, with its outlet or a map.
3. **The data you hold.** Heads, discharge, geology, pumping, and the licence of each.
4. **The time frame and what you will do with the answer.**

Observed data such as a gauged discharge or a mapped stream network are used to compare the
model with the field and to calibrate it. They are not inputs of the model. The
{doc}`Understand </understand/index>` pages explain what a result shows and what it does not.
